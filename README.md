# Favorite Series: Manhwa with Mystery and Meta Magic

Welcome to my curated list of favorite manhwa-style series that blend mystery, fantasy, and mind-bending storytelling. These titles are perfect for fans of complex world-building, layered characters, and plots that keep you guessing.

---

## Featured Titles

### 1. **Lord of the Mysteries**
> *"The world is a book, and those who do not travel read only one page."*

- **Author**: Cuttlefish That Loves Diving
- **Genre**: Steampunk, Mystery, Supernatural
- **Themes**:
  - Secret organizations
  - Tarot-inspired powers
  - Victorian-era aesthetics
- **Why I love it**:
  - Deep lore and mythos
  - Unpredictable plot twists
  - A protagonist who ascends from ordinary to godlike

### 2. **Omniscient Reader’s Viewpoint**
> *"I only know the end of the world. But I want to change the story."*

- **Authors**: Sing Shong (writing duo)
- **Genre**: Action, Psychological, Fantasy
- **Themes**:
  - Meta-fiction and reader-as-hero
  - Apocalypse survival
  - Character-driven drama
- **Why I love it**:
  - Clever use of narrative perspective
  - Emotional depth and character growth
  - Epic battles with strategic twists
